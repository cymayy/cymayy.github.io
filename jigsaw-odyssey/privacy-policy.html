Jigsaw Odyssey Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android app Jigsaw Odyssey (package name com.jigsawodyssey.app) handles information. The app is published by Editing Tools Lab (“we”, “us”).

This policy applies to the version of Jigsaw Odyssey distributed on Google Play that contains no advertising. If a future version adds advertising or any other service that processes personal data, we will update this policy before that version is released.

2. Summary

  • Jigsaw Odyssey does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • The app does not request the Android permission needed to access the internet. It cannot send your game data to us or to anyone else.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software development kits (SDKs), and no in-app purchases.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following game data in its private storage on your device:

DataPurpose
Puzzle progress (piece positions, number of moves, elapsed time) and completed levelsResume unfinished puzzles and unlock the next levels
In-game Points balanceSpend Points on in-game hints
Cosmic Odyssey journey progress (in-game energy, milestones, discovered content, tutorial status)Show your journey and collection
Sound effects setting (on/off)Remember your preference
A small internal app-state recordInternal app configuration; it contains no personal information

This data is created by your gameplay. It is not linked to your identity, and it is not transmitted to us. We do not have access to it.

4. Information we collect, use and share

We do not collect personal information through the app, and we do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties.

The app does not request the internet permission or any permission that gives access to personal data on your device. It does not read device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, and it does not access your photos, media, files, camera, microphone, location, contacts or accounts.

5. Contacting support

The app’s Settings screen can open your own email app so that you can write to us. If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this information only to reply to you and to handle your request, and we do not share it with anyone else.

We keep support emails only for as long as we need them to respond to you and handle your request. You can ask us at any time to delete your emails, and we will do so.

The Settings screen can also open this Privacy Policy in your web browser. Your browser, not Jigsaw Odyssey, handles that connection.

6. Third-party services

Jigsaw Odyssey does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information about your device and your use of Google Play under Google’s own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics information with app developers on your device, Google may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

7. Backups

Depending on your device settings, Android may include the app’s saved game data in your device backup (for example, a backup stored in your Google Account) or copy it when you transfer data to a new device. These backups are provided and controlled by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot access them. You can manage backups in your device settings.

8. Data security

Game data is kept in the app’s private storage area, which Android protects from access by other apps. Because the app does not transmit this data, it is not exposed to transmission over a network. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all of it at any time by either:

  • uninstalling Jigsaw Odyssey, or
  • opening Android Settings → Apps → Jigsaw Odyssey → Storage and choosing Clear storage (the exact wording depends on your device).

Deleting game data cannot be undone. If Android backup is turned on, a copy may remain in your device backup and may be restored when you reinstall the app. To request deletion of support emails, contact us at the address below.

10. Children

Jigsaw Odyssey is intended for users aged 6 and older. The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children. It has no accounts, chat or other social features, advertising or in-app purchases. The only links that leave the app are in Settings: this Privacy Policy, which opens in a web browser, and our support email address, which opens in an email app.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app’s features change. We will post the updated policy at this address and change the effective date above. If a change significantly affects how information is handled, we will also announce it in the app or on its Google Play page before the change takes effect.
